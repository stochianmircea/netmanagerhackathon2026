## What is being compared

Three things decide on the same 24 states, drawn from 3 sites:

1. **A fixed-threshold rule** (`tools/baseline.php`). Deterministic, instant, free.
   It carries 20 lines of hand-written per-site knowledge
   (~7 per site): the threshold, the order in which loads may be
   cut, and the list of devices that must never be touched. Every new installation
   means writing another block by hand and keeping it in step with reality.
2. **What the model proposed** — every plan it wrote, including the ones the validator
   threw out whole.
3. **What the system would have executed** — only the runs the validator accepted.

The distance between 2 and 3 is the brake. Reporting only 3 would flatter the model;
reporting only 2 would slander the system. Both are below.

## What this does not show

- It does not show behaviour on real hardware. These are recorded states, not a live site.
- It does not show behaviour outside the 24 states in `fixtures/bench/states/`.
- It does not show that the rule is a bad rule. On most days the rule is right, it is
  instant, and it costs nothing to run. What it cannot do is read a policy: its
  knowledge is the 20 hand-written lines counted above, and every
  new site needs more of them.
- Watts are the expected saving each plan declares for itself, cross-checked against the
  state's inventory. They are not metered.
